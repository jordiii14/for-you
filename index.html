for my wifey.

My wifey,

I don't know if I'll ever be able to perfectly explain what you mean to me. Sometimes I just know that having you in my life makes ordinary days feel a little less ordinary.

I know life isn't always easy. There are days when you're tired, when your mind is full, when things don't go the way you wanted, and when you just need someone to remind you that you don't have to carry everything alone.

So whenever you need that reminder, come back here. Read this slowly and remember that somewhere out there is your husband who is very, very grateful for you.

I want to be someone who makes you feel safe to be yourself. Someone who listens. Someone who stays. Someone who celebrates your wins and sits beside you through the difficult parts.

And yes, I also want to steal your attention occasionally, make you blush for absolutely no reason, and remind you that your husband still thinks you're ridiculously cute.

Thank you for being you, wifey. That's enough. More than enough, actually.

I love you, wifey.
— hubby
